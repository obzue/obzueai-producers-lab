## 🎹 Overview

**ObzueAI PRODUCER'S LAB** is an enterprise-grade, full-stack Digital Audio Workstation (DAW) and autonomous music production environment developed by **ObzueAI Enterprise**.

By combining low-latency open-source audio engines with **100 VST Virtual Instruments**, **500 Sound Sources & DSP Modules**, a universal **USB MIDI driver subsystem**, and an **Autonomous Agentic AI Co-Producer**, ObzueAI PRODUCER'S LAB transforms raw creative sparks into fully mastered albums, 4K music videos, viral social content, and legal publishing assets.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- **🎼 Single-Tone & Hum-to-Song Synthesis**: Generate complete, multi-track studio productions starting from a single frequency, hummed melody, or text cue across any global genre.
- **🤖 World-Class AI Co-Producer**: An agentic conversational creative partner that monitors session dynamics, detects spectral masking, auto-applies gain staging (-14 LUFS / -1.0 dB True Peak), and executes arrangement decisions.
- **🎛️ 100 Integrated VST Instruments**: Built-in hybrid wavetable, subtractive, 6-operator FM, and granular synths powered by Surge XT, Dexed, Helm, and VCV Rack engines.
- **🎚️ 500 DSP Sound & FX Modules**: Dynamic processors including multiband EQs, limiters, noise gates, Dattorro reverbs, saturators, sine/oscillating synths, and drum machines.
- **🔌 Universal USB/WebMIDI Hotplug Drivers**: Zero-configuration driver layer mapping pitch, velocity, and CC parameters for any USB MIDI hardware controller.
- **🎬 Full Album & Media Suite Generation**: Automated rendering of 4000x4000 album artwork, 4K cinematic AI music videos, and viral short-form clips tailored for TikTok and YouTube Shorts.
- **📜 Legal, Copyright & Publishing Suite**: Auto-generates work-for-hire split sheets, ISRC/ISWC metadata codes, ASCAP/BMI registration data, and sync licensing agreements.
